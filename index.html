contrail

Every flight, from pushback to touchdown.

Contrail is a free Android flight tracker: live positions, take-off and landing alerts, times in each airport's own zone, and your lifetime of flying. No account, no ads — everything stays on your phone.

Where the data comes from

Community ADS-B networks (adsb.lol, adsb.fi, airplanes.live, OpenSky Network), NOAA aviation weather and FAA airport status. Airports and airlines from OpenFlights; maps from Natural Earth.

Shared a flight with you? Open the link on your phone and it will open in Contrail.
